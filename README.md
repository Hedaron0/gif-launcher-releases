# GIF Launcher

A Windows app that puts GIFs, short clips and sounds on your friends' screens, at the same moment as yours. Not in a chat: on the screen, in a small window over whatever they are doing.

**[Download for Windows](https://github.com/Hedaron0/gif-launcher-releases/releases/latest/download/GIF-Launcher-Setup.exe)** · [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PH28TTPNL6L) · [hedaron.com/gif-launcher](https://hedaron.com/gif-launcher)

## How it works

1. You and your friends install GIF Launcher (Windows 10 or 11).
2. One of you makes a channel and shares the invite link.
3. Paste a link or search, press Play. It opens on every screen in the channel.

Step by step, with the keys: [hedaron.com/gif-launcher/guide](https://hedaron.com/gif-launcher/guide)

## What it does

- **Plays on their screen, not in a chat.** A GIF, clip or sound pops up on top of whatever your friends are doing, at the same moment for everyone.
- **Channels and invite links.** No account. Share a link and you are in.
- **Search built in.** GIFs, videos and sounds from the web, or paste a link.
- **Keys for your favourites.** Give a reaction a key and send it while a game has the screen.
- **Talk while you send.** Text chat and voice inside the channel.
