# GIF Launcher

Send GIFs, clips and sounds straight onto your friend's screen, in sync, without opening a chat.

**[Download for Windows](https://github.com/Hedaron0/gif-launcher-releases/releases/latest/download/GIF-Launcher-Setup.exe)** · [hedaron.com/gif-launcher](https://hedaron.com/gif-launcher)

## What it does

- **Plays on their screen, not in a chat.** A GIF, clip or sound pops up on top of whatever your friends are doing, at the same moment for everyone.
- **Channels and invite codes.** No account. Share a code or a link and you are in.
- **Search built in.** GIFs, videos and sounds from the web, or paste any link.
- **Talk while you send.** Text chat and voice inside the channel.
- **Made to stay out of the way.** Quiet in the background, themes you can make your own.
